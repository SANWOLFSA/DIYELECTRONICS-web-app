Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

DIY Electronics (diyelectronics.beehivehub.co.za) is run by Beehive Hub (Pty) Ltd, South Africa. This page explains what data we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you create an account or sign in with Google or Facebook, we store your name, email address and profile photo. We also store your profile, progress, the agreements you accept, and the posts, guides, listings, orders and settings you create. Some settings are kept in your browser on your device.

How we use it

We use your data to run your account and the app's features. Questions, photos and videos you send to the AI assistant go to Google's Gemini AI service to create an answer. We use Google Analytics (Firebase) to count page visits so we can improve the app.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in Google Firebase.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Content you post publicly (videos, guides, posts, listings, reviews) can be seen by other users. Buyers and shops see the order details they need to complete an order.

Facebook Login

If you sign in with Facebook, we only receive your public profile (name and profile photo) and email address. We do not post to Facebook for you.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time. South African users have rights under POPIA; users elsewhere may have similar rights under their own law. See how to delete your data.
