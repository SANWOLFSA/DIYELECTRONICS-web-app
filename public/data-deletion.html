How to delete your data

You can ask us to delete your DIY Electronics account and all data linked to it, including data from signing in with Facebook or Google.

Steps

  1. Send an email to diy.electronicsa@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use in the app, or tell us that address.
  3. We delete your account, profile and personal data within 30 days and email you when it's done.

Remove Facebook access

You can also remove the app from Facebook: open Facebook, go to Settings & privacy, Settings, Apps and websites, then remove DIY Electronics.

Some records may be kept where the law requires it, such as order receipts.
